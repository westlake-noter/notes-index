Personal study notes by **Jiete XUE** for the Fall 2025 Quantum Mechanics
course at Westlake University, taught by **Congjun Wu**.

- Repository: [quantum-mechanics-notes](https://github.com/JieteXue/quantum-mechanics-notes)

- Source: `quantum-mechanics-notes.tex`, with illustrations in `figures/`.

## Overview

The notes cover quantum foundations and operator methods, wave functions and
dynamics, the harmonic oscillator, gauge transformations, angular momentum,
discrete symmetries, perturbation theory, path integrals, and scattering.
The separate **TOC** tab follows the compiled PDF's numbered contents and page
references, rather than the broader topic grouping in the repository README.

These are independently reorganized personal notes, not official lecture notes
or a complete record of the course. Their order differs from the lectures,
and they may contain errors or omissions.
